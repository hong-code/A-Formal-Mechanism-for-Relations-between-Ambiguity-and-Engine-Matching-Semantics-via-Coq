\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{JavaScript regex semantics.}
Barriere et al. provide the closest semantic foundation: a complete and
mechanized JavaScript regex semantics that produces ordered backtracking trees
and is proved faithful to the ECMAScript specification via Warblre
equivalence~\cite{barriere2026jsregex}.  Our work is complementary.  Rather
than verifying an engine algorithm such as PikeVM, we use their tree perspective
as the engine-side semantic object to be related to automata ambiguity.

\paragraph{Automata ambiguity.}
Weber and Seidl's work is the classical source for structural analysis of NFA
ambiguity~\cite{weber1991ambiguity}.  Their criteria for infinite ambiguity and
polynomial ambiguity growth are especially relevant because they turn a semantic
growth question into a finite graph problem.  Our contribution is to position
these criteria as certificates for backtracking trace growth.

\paragraph{ReDoS analysis.}
Existing ReDoS tools typically search for syntactic vulnerability patterns or
construct witness strings for catastrophic backtracking.  The mechanism proposed
here aims at a proof-producing alternative: a detector should justify its
warning by an automata ambiguity certificate and a verified connection to the
engine semantics.  The approach is not yet a complete detector, but it clarifies
which semantic property a detector should certify.
